## Abstract

TravelTrust is a decentralized travel booking protocol stack focused on **on-chain Escrow, verifiable workflows, and reputation**. This document describes an **optional protocol governance token** intended for **parameter governance and ecosystem budgeting**, **not** as the pricing or default settlement asset for travel services.

**Current phase**: The protocol may **never** issue a governance token until product and regulatory assessment complete. **MVP does not issue a platform payment token**; order settlement uses allowlisted stable assets (see 01). This document is **forward-looking** and does not constitute an offer of securities or virtual assets in any jurisdiction.

---

## 1. Role of the token (illustrative)

| Dimension | Statement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Protocol role | Voting / delegation on **governance scope** only (as finalized in charter) |
| Not payment | **Not** the quote or Escrow settlement currency for travel orders |
| Not equity | **No** representation of corporate equity; **no** promised dividends unless separate legal opinion and disclosure |
| Staking | Guide **performance staking** may use a different asset (e.g. USDC); see [81 (ZH)](../../81-经济模型-向导质押与订单押金.md) |

## 2. Risks (non-exhaustive)

Regulatory classification, smart contract risk, market volatility, governance attacks, roadmap change including **no token issuance**.

## 3. Compliance boundary

No guaranteed returns; no fixed profit linkage to fees or corporate profits; wording must align with **08-4** and Terms of Service.

If you describe **protocol fee routing** in public materials: the **allocatable platform fee 100%** denominator and the fact that **arbitration fees** and **guide performance stake slash** are **orthogonal** to the FeeRouter **45/55** split are SSOT in **[84 §1.1.1 (ZH)](../../84-第一阶段10国Country-Pool发行参数总表.md)** and **[Runbook §7.1](../../../../ops/RUNBOOK.md)**; legal must rewrite for external tone—do **not** blend slash/arbitration into the same percentage story as 45/55 without a footnote.
