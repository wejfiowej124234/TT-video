## Policy (approved)

**Public Catalog = one Official Production Dataset (OCS).**  
**Database ≠ Public Catalog** — Archive, Draft, Test, Demo, Smoke, Historical, Canonical rows **may remain in DB**; they must **not** appear on Public Catalog.

## Operational discipline

- Update OCS entities **in place** (same city chain / same IDs)
- Do **not** create parallel published entities per city (e.g. 东京摄影师2)
